A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Design is more than how it looks.

I'm a Pretoria-based creative designer with a background in Creative Technology and Interactive Development. My interests have grown towards graphic design, communication design and visual storytelling.

I enjoy taking a brief, finding the idea at its centre and translating it into visuals that feel clear, considered and memorable. I'm especially interested in the worlds of sport, entertainment, brands and culture.
